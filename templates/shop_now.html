About Us
Our Vision The vision of the Rebottle Rewards project is to promote sustainable recycling by incentivizing
users to recycle plastic bottles through an innovative reward system.
By integrating QR code-based authentication, fraud detection, and a points-based redemption system, the project aims
to encourage eco-friendly habits while reducing plastic waste and fostering a greener future
Our Approach The Rebottle Rewards project follows a QR code-based authentication approach, where users scan a unique QR code to connect with the recycling machine. The system validates plastic bottle deposits, awards points for each recycled bottle, and enables users to redeem rewards for eco-friendly products. Secure authentication, fraud detection, and seamless reward tracking ensure a smooth and
transparent experience.
Our Process Users log in to the Rebottle Rewards website and scan the QR code displayed on the recycling machine.
After depositing plastic bottles, the system validates the input, detects fraud, and awards 5 credit points per bottle.
Users can then redeem their accumulated points for eco-friendly products through the platform.
How ReBottle Rewards work ?
Collection &
Sorting Plastic bottles are collected and sorted based on type and quality.
Cleaning & Shredding Bottles are washed to remove labels, glue, and impurities, then shredded into plastic flakes.
Melting & Repurposing The shredded plastic is melted and reformed into pellets or filaments.
Additives may be introduced to improve biodegradability.
Biodegradable Formation The processed plastic is mixed with natural biodegradable materials like starch, cellulose, or plant fibers.
Molding & Product Creation Recycled plastic is processed into biodegradable and commercial products through molding and extrusion techniques.
Quality Testing & Distribution Finished products are tested for durability and biodegradability before distribution.
